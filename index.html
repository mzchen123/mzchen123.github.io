👋 Mengzhuo Chen

I am currently a PhD student at Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences, the National Engineering Research Center for Software Engineering. My research interest is broadly in Foundational LLMs, LLM Agents and Reliable AI/Systems. I am dedicated to bridging the gap between AI and systems in the real world, and making AI and systems more reliable.
